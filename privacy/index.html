How we keep your email.

Short and plain, because it should be.

Who we are

Team Automata, the people making Automata Framework. Write to us at hello@automataframework.com.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • The editor you'd use first, if you picked one.
  • When you joined.
  • To stop floods of fake sign-ups: a scrambled code made from your internet address. It can't be turned back into the address, and we delete it the next day.

Why

To tell you when Automata Framework is ready, and to count which editor people want first. We only keep it because you asked us to, and you can change your mind at any time.

Where

With Cloudflare, which runs this site, stores the list and sends our emails. Their servers may be outside your country.

How long

Until you leave the list. If you never tap the link in our first email, we delete your address within 7 days.

What we never do

Sell it, share it, or use it for ads. This site has no tracking and sets no cookies.

How to leave

Tap "Leave the list" in any email from us, and your address is deleted straight away. Or write to us, and we'll delete it, or tell you what we hold about you.
